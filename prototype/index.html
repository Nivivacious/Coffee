The only two cards without a mirror

The Departure and The Return

Funny how you can miss two places at once.

Every other card turns. These two can't, because a pour only goes down. Each Joker pulls coffee from one vessel to the other the way a darshini cools and froths it. The person who lives between the two cities is the stream, and the travel is what makes it right.

On The Departure the sentence flows downstream, from the steel tumbler into a New York paper cup. On The Return it runs upstream, from a davara that someone kept a little coffee in. The Return's stream is paler. You come back with more milk in you.

The other 51 cards share rotational symmetry: the same either way up. The Jokers are mirror images of each other, so they only make a whole shape side by side.

How the deck works

Ranks keep time

Laid out from 2 to 10, each suit is one day, and every card's caption gives the hour. Aces are origins. Jacks are the people who keep the city moving, Queens are what the city loves, and Kings are what it complains about and never fixes.

Inks

Seven spot colours on warm ivory stock, printed like a screen print. Marigold is shared: the same ink makes the auto's hood and the cab.

Print and motion

Every card is vector SVG on an exact 2.5 × 3.5 in grid, with a 0.125 in bleed version for the printer. The animation lives in the file. For the printed deck, the seam band can be lenticular, so tilting the card in your hand plays the motion.
